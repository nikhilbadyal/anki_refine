# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Daily Anki learners, mostly language learners (Japanese especially), who open Anki every day to clear their reviews. They come to it in short, repeated sessions: at a desk before work, in the evening, often tired. The job: see at a glance what is waiting, start studying with one action, get through the cards, and leave. They already know Anki; they want it to feel good to come back to, not to learn a new tool.

## Product Purpose

Refine is an Anki Desktop add-on that gives Anki's everyday screens (home, deck overview, reviewer answer bar, statistics, menus) a calm, modern interface while leaving Anki's behaviour, scheduling, card templates and data untouched. Success: opening Anki → knowing what to do → studying takes fewer decisions and feels pleasant; nothing breaks, cards look exactly as their templates intend, and turning Refine off returns stock Anki.

## Brand Personality

Calm, focused, crafted. Quiet warm surfaces where nothing shouts; the cards and the "cards waiting" number are the loudest things on screen. Details feel considered (spacing, type, states) in the way Linear and Raycast feel considered, without copying them. Tone of copy: plain, short, encouraging without cheerleading.

## Anti-references

- Gamified study apps: streak fireworks, mascots, coins, confetti, badges (the Duolingo register).
- SaaS dashboards: hero-metric tiles, gradient accents, identical card grids, chart walls.
- Heavy "theme" add-ons: wallpapers, glassmorphism, neon, big decorative backgrounds.
- Anything that restyles card content without being asked; note templates own the card.

## Design Principles

1. **The study path comes first.** Every screen should make "what's waiting → study" the most obvious action; secondary information stays secondary.
2. **Restyle, don't replace.** Anki's behaviour, shortcuts and data are sacred; Refine changes how things look and where controls live, never what they do.
3. **Quiet by default.** One accent, warm neutrals, motion that settles rather than performs; emphasis is earned by importance.
4. **Honest numbers.** Every figure comes from Anki's own data with Anki's own definitions; no invented metrics.
5. **Off means stock.** Every surface can be turned off live and leaves no trace.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA: text contrast ≥ 4.5:1 (≥ 3:1 for large text and UI boundaries), full keyboard operation with visible focus, colour never the only signal (Again/Good carry labels), reduced motion respected (Refine's Motion setting plus the OS preference).
